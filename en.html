How I triage a suspicious e-mail

  1. PyThreatIntel reads Authentication-Results

    The topmost Authentication-Results is the one header the sender cannot write. SPF, DKIM and DMARC come from there.

  2. PyThreatIntel traces the origin IP

    The Received chain is read bottom to top until the first public IP. Private ranges are left out.

  3. PyThreatIntel writes the JSON report

    URLs, SHA-256 hashes of attachments and the VirusTotal reputation go into a report a SIEM can read.
